# Artist Daily Practice

A flexible Markdown journal system for artists and independent creatives.

Creative work is not only finished output. It also includes experiments, failed attempts, material observations, rehearsals, research, changing judgments, preparation, maintenance, and recovery. Artist Daily Practice helps preserve that process without turning an artistic life into a productivity dashboard.

## Who it is for

The templates are designed to be medium-agnostic. They can be adapted by visual artists, sculptors, ceramic artists, photographers, filmmakers, performers, dancers, musicians, sound artists, writers, designers, illustrators, and interdisciplinary practitioners.

## Start here

- Use [`templates/five-minute-log.md`](templates/five-minute-log.md) for a low-friction daily check-in.
- Use [`templates/full-practice-review.md`](templates/full-practice-review.md) when you want to document experiments, decisions, difficulties, and the state of a project in more depth.
- See [`examples/filled-example.md`](examples/filled-example.md) for a fictional example.

The files are plain Markdown. Copy them into Obsidian, another notes app, or any folder where you keep your practice journal. No plugin is required.

## Principles

- Record what happened before interpreting it.
- Separate direct observation from interpretation.
- Do not treat finished output as the only form of progress.
- Preserve useful failures, uncertainty, pauses, and changes in judgment.
- Do not force every day to produce insight or significance.
- Complete only the sections that are useful; remove the rest.

## Suggested workflow

1. Choose the short or full template according to the day.
2. Write concrete actions and observations first.
3. Record interpretation only where it is genuinely useful.
4. End with one concrete next step—or explicitly decide to leave the work alone.
5. Review several entries together to notice recurring questions and working conditions.

## Customize it

You are encouraged to rename fields, remove sections, and add medium-specific prompts. A dancer may add rehearsal and physical-load fields; a ceramic artist may add drying and firing states; a filmmaker may add footage, sound, and edit-version fields.

The generic template is a foundation, not a form that must be completed.
